## What kind of live show suits your property?

### Restaurant and dining hall
A solo acoustic singer with guitar, or a duo with keyboard and cajon, is ideal. The volume stays comfortable for conversation, and the setlist can move from retro Bollywood to new hits.

### Bonfire and lawn evenings
In winter, bonfire nights are a favourite with families and groups. A singer who knows Pahari folk songs, old Hindi classics and a few Punjabi numbers keeps everyone singing along.

### Weekend Sufi or ghazal night
For premium properties, a Sufi or ghazal evening with harmonium and tabla creates a calm, special mood that suits Shimla perfectly.

### Peak season and New Year
For Christmas, New Year's Eve and long weekends, a full band with sound and lights turns your hall or terrace into the place to be.

## How much does a live singer for a hotel in Shimla cost?

Prices depend on the act, the duration and the season. As a rough guide:

- **Solo acoustic singer (2 hours):** usually starts around ₹8,000
- **Duo (singer with keyboard or cajon, 3 hours):** higher, depending on sound needs
- **Full band with sound and lights:** a bigger budget, especially on New Year's Eve and peak weekends

## Checklist before you book a hotel singer

1. **Listen first.** Ask for real performance videos, not only studio recordings.
2. **Confirm the setlist style.** Bollywood, Sufi, Pahari, English acoustic or a mix?
3. **Sound system.** Check whether the singer brings their own sound, or uses yours.
4. **Timing and breaks.** Agree on start time, set length and short breaks.
5. **Backup plan.** Roads to Kufri and Narkanda can close in snowfall. Ask what happens if the singer cannot reach.
6. **Invoice.** Hotels and companies usually need a proper invoice for accounts.
