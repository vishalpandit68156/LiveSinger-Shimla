A wedding in the hills is special. Pine trees, cool evenings and a view over the valley make Shimla, Kufri, Mashobra and Chail some of the most loved wedding destinations in North India. The right **wedding singer in Shimla** turns those evenings into memories your family talks about for years.

Here is how to plan live music for each function, and how to book a singer you can trust.

## Live music for every wedding function

### Haldi and mehndi
These are relaxed, daytime functions. A **Sufi or acoustic singer** with dholak or guitar works beautifully. Think soft Bollywood, Punjabi folk songs and family favourites that everyone can join in on.

### Sangeet night
This is where a full **singer live show** shines. A Bollywood or Punjabi band with sound and lights gets everyone on the dance floor. Ask the singer to learn one or two special songs for the couple's entry or family performances.

### Wedding day and pheras
Some families like a devotional or classical singer during the pheras, or gentle instrumental music while guests arrive.

### Reception
A mix of retro classics, romantic Bollywood and light Sufi keeps the mood elegant while guests eat and greet the couple.

### Add a local touch: Pahari folk
Guests from outside Himachal love a short **Pahari naati** or folk performance. It is a lovely way to welcome them to the hills.

## Questions to ask before you book

1. **Have you performed at weddings in Shimla before?** Venue experience matters in the hills.
2. **Can you share a full live performance video?** Short clips can hide a lot.
3. **Is sound included, and is it enough for an open lawn?**
4. **Can you learn our special songs?** Ask how much notice they need.
5. **What happens if the road is blocked or you fall sick?** In winter this is a real risk. A platform with a backup singer protects your day.
6. **What is the payment schedule?** A small advance and the rest after the show is fair for both sides.
